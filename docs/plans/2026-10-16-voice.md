## 1. What this plan is

The spec asks for eight components (its §4, plus the mobile relay of §9). Read against the tree (design §1), they fall into four groups, and the plan's ordering follows from that:

1. **The conversation half is one spawn and a parser this tree half-has** (design F1, F38, D40, D54). The bridge comes **first** (phase V1) and it comes **without audio** — text in, text out, on screen — because a bridge built after the audio would be shaped by it, and because everything in phase V1 is testable with a fake child and a scripted transcript. **It is also the phase that takes the write**: the **helper process** runs the Agent SDK under the operator's identity, and D54's containment — the SDK's own permission callbacks, plus a deny list the operator writes in their own settings — is the whole of what stands between a misheard turn and a real `fugaro cancel` (F38). **D40's answer of 2026-10-16 is the mechanism this phase is written for** — the Agent SDK in a **separate helper process**, not a spawned `claude -p` child — and the exception that keeps the app credential-free is **D54b**, answered on 2026-10-16 in the words that row records, so **phase V1's blocker is no longer a decision**: it is the **amendment the console design must receive** (voice.md §5.4, **owned by Task V0.4**) and the console's own shell, and the gate and Task V1.1 both say so.
2. **The event half is a classification layer over a document that already exists** (design F2, F3, D48). It comes second (phase V2) because it depends on nothing else and because it is the cheapest part of the spec: `fugaro watch --json` already computes the burn rate, the cap fractions and the kill state, and the console already reads them through `FugaroSource`.
3. **The audio is where the credentials, the permissions and the provider choice live** (design F15, F18, F19, F22; D43–D46). It comes third, behind its own decisions, and behind interfaces with fakes so that four of the spec's six acceptance criteria are asserted in CI without a microphone and without a provider. **With D44 and D46 answered, the v1 audio path is local-only and measured**: phase V4 wires the local provider (V4.4) against the bake-off's own numbers, and no cloud call is on it.
4. **Everything that writes, everything that leaves the machine, and everything that needs a new artifact comes last and separately** (phases V7–V10): the cloud providers, the packaged permission ask, the standalone entry point, and — if ever — the phone relay, which is a rules change and a design of its own. **Two of those four are now answered rather than open, and both answers shorten v1**: the destructive commands are **not in v1** at all (D47, so V6.3 is deferred and phase V6 ships no write path), and the cloud providers are **out of v1** (D44 and D46, so phase V7 happens only if the owner takes a second, explicit egress decision).

**Releases.** The console is its own artifact published from the CLI's own `vX.Y.Z` tag (console D11), and this module ships inside that artifact rather than as a second one (design D50). **This plan adds no release and is not scheduled against one** (design D53). The release train this repository records is **R4 of [2026-10-14-project-centric.md](2026-10-14-project-centric.md) as the owner's ruling of 2026-10-15 refined it: 0.8.0 is cut after project-centric phases 1 and 2, and phases 3 to 6 moved to 0.9.0** — recorded on `main` in [compute-backend.md](../design/compute-backend.md) §6.2 (*"The owner's release ruling of 2026-10-15 cut 0.8.0 after project-centric phases 1 and 2"*) and in [gcp-live-checklist.md](../gcp-live-checklist.md) (*"Phase 1 ships in 0.8.0, and phases 3 to 6 ship in 0.9.0"*), while that plan's own R4 sentence — *"phases 1 to 6 land on `next`, released together as 0.8.0"* — is the *before* of the ruling rather than a competing claim. **0.8.0 is tagged**: the remote carries `refs/tags/v0.8.0`, [docs/releases/v0.8.0.md](../releases/v0.8.0.md) is on `main`, its notes are the project-centric phases 1 and 2, and the desktop console is named in that file's *"Not in this release"* line — so that train has left, and **the voice work is post-0.8.0 and post-console, and unscheduled** (design D53): it ships inside the console's artifact, and the console's first signed build has not shipped. **The console is its own artifact published from the CLI's own tag (console D11), and its work lands on its own branch, `console`** — `refs/heads/console` on the remote at **`c7572646`** (2026-10-16), fifteen commits past the v2 reconciliation's `2172d92`, carrying the console's **Task 1.1** shell (the exactly pinned toolchain, the hardened window and the `app://` document it draws, with `npm run test:unit` and `test:e2e`) and **not** the harness this plan's tasks name: there is no spawn table, no `FugaroSource` reader and no fixture tree on it. Its phase-0 commits are on `main` too. Which branch the console grows in changes nothing this plan depends on — it depends on the console's shell existing, and §3's prerequisite states what is still missing. **The highest release document on `main` is [docs/releases/v0.8.0.md](../releases/v0.8.0.md)**, and `refs/tags/v0.8.0` is on the remote — so the v0.7.5 an earlier revision of this paragraph named is no longer the most recent one.

**Global constraints.** The console plan's Global Constraints **1–10** apply to every task here, verbatim and without restatement: no new backend ever; no credential read, held or stored by the app; the app never writes; the renderer is untrusted and gets no network; every number carries an availability state; `belong` is never touched; no `.github/workflows/` edit by an agent (stage under `docs/pending-workflows/`); the Go module is untouched; SECURITY-SENSITIVE tasks are not merged on a self-review; docs-only tasks keep the docs suite green with `GOFLAGS=-p=2 go test ./internal/cli -run 'Docs|Lint' -count=1`. Five more are this plan's own:

11. **A credential value exists in exactly two places and no others**: the store that owns it on the operator's machine (their keychain, or Claude Code's own login store for the Claude credential of D42's recommendation) and the memory of the process that resolves it — the helper process D40's answer puts the SDK in, or a spawned child if the owner's answer ever puts one back — for as long as that process runs. **D40's answer is the reason this constraint names a process rather than a child, and D54b — answered on 2026-10-16 — is the row that says exactly how narrow the exception is**: the helper alone, the login resolved at runtime and never stored or logged, and the renderer and the main process proved to have no path to it (voice.md §5.4 lists the amendment that carries the exception into the console design). Nowhere else: not this repository, not `fugaro.yaml`, not a published shared config, not the console's settings document, not the app, not a log, not a fixture, not a test.
12. **Nothing recorded from a person is committed.** Not a voice, not a transcript of a real conversation. Audio fixtures are **synthesised** (Task V4.3) and transcripts come from fixtures that pass D6's redaction check. This is tighter than the console plan's rule 6 and it is deliberate: design F32. **Note added 2026-10-19, and this constraint is not relaxed:** the mobile evaluation switch ([docs/mobile/design/voice-evaluation-switch.md](../mobile/design/voice-evaluation-switch.md) §1a, the owner's answer to its Q0 on 2026-10-19) uploads live audio to an allow-listed provider under its own scope; **it changes nothing here** — the fixtures this work commits stay synthesised, no recording of a person is committed by it, and its own never-send rules keep no recording beyond the live stream.
13. **Every audible claim is traceable to a document or a fake.** A spoken sentence may state a fact only if that fact is a field of a `watch --json` document, a field of an agent message, or a value the summarizer was given — and the digest may never invent a state (`important`'s "awaiting user input" is dropped until something defines it: design F3, D48). This is the console's §6.1 availability rule with a microphone in front of it, and it is a review criterion, not a nicety.
14. **The helper's spawn table is as mutation-proof as the one-binary table.** Console §5.1 rule 6's mechanism (a test that fails when a command or an argument is added) applies to the helper's table verbatim, and it carries its own never-list — including `--dangerously-skip-permissions` and the SDK's equivalent permission-skipping configuration, which the runner passes in a disposable container (`internal/agent/agent.go`'s `Args`) and which is a different proposition on the operator's own machine (design D40, D54, U5).
15. **The app's table fences the app's argv and nothing else, and no document in this plan pretends otherwise.** The **helper's agent** runs under the operator's identity with the operator's tools; `fugaro cancel`, `fugaro budget kill`, `gh pr merge` and any other command are reachable by the agent itself, and the spec's §8 assumes it. Every claim in this plan about writes is either about **the app** (which writes nothing: console Global Constraint 3) or about **the helper** (whose agent can, and whose reach D54's permission callbacks and the operator's deny list are what limits). A test that asserts "no destructive command can be spawned" must assert it of **the app's tables** and must not be worded as if it covered the agent — this is the wording Task V6.3 fixes.

**Numbering, so nothing here collides.** These tasks are **V0.1 onward** and they are this document's own namespace. Two other namespaces exist and neither is touched: the console plan's tasks (0.1–5.x) and the console v2 plan's (V2.0.1 onward, merged as #426). Design decisions continue from **D39** and the reason is stated in [voice.md](../design/voice.md) §2 — D1–D20 are answered on `main`, **D21–D38 are merged on `main` too** (the console v2 reconciliation, #426, `2172d92`, 2026-10-16), and the tree's own rule for a collision is "whichever merges second renumbers" — which this plan avoids by starting at D39 rather than reopening a merged range. **The numbers beyond that range are not this work's**: the same owner allocation gives **D55 onward to managed projects (#429)** ([desktop-console-v2.md](../design/desktop-console-v2.md) §2, "managed projects (#429) takes D55 onward"), so the one extra row this work needs — Task V4.2's permission row — is **reserved and not yet written as D54a**, a lettered number inside this work's own block, the convention `project-centric.md` §6.2 uses for **D1a**/**D1b** and the check register uses for **59a**/**59b**. **An earlier revision reserved D55 for it, which is #429's block and not this work's; the correction is recorded rather than quietly applied.** No task may write a decision into `docs/design/desktop-console.md`, whose table ends at D20 on `main`.

## 3. Phases and tasks

Each task is independently mergeable: it lands with its own tests, and **a phase's merge must not need anything from a later phase** — the dependencies run forward, and §6 lists every one of them so a reviewer can check the claim rather than take it. (An earlier revision said "nothing in phase *n* is required by phase *n−1*'s merge", which is true of almost any ordering and says nothing about the risk: the risk is a task that cannot land until something after it lands.)

**One prerequisite is neither a decision nor a task in this plan: the console itself has to exist.** Read from `origin/main`, `console/` holds exactly two files — `console/README.md` and `console/docs/json-contract.md` — and nothing else: **there is no `FugaroSource`, no spawn table, no fixture tree and no console test command on `main`**, because the console plan's phase 0 (the contract and the boundary) is the merged one and phases 1–4 are not built. So every task here that says "the console's own test command", "the console's own eight fixture cases" or "the console's `FugaroSource`" means **the harness the console plan's phase 1 (or phase 4, for the packaged build) delivers**, and until that exists the task has no test command to run. **That branch has begun the work without delivering the harness**: `refs/heads/console` is at `c7572646` and its Task 1.1 commits land the pinned toolchain, the hardened window, the `app://` document and the unit and e2e commands — but `console/src/main/spawn.ts`, the `FugaroSource` reader and the fixture tree are **not on it**, and neither is any of it on `main`, so the rule below and the list it yields are unchanged. **The dependency is a rule, not a list: every task whose `Verification:` names the console's test command depends on console phases 1–4**, because that command is the harness the console's phase 1 delivers and the packaged build its phase 4 delivers. **V6.3 — destructive commands: DEFERRED by D47, and the part of it that is not deferred. (needs D47 — answered 2026-10-16 — and D54) — SECURITY-SENSITIVE**
**D47's answer is "not in v1", so this task is deferred out of v1 and out of phase V6**: no design document for a voice-driven destructive command is written by this plan, no command is added to any table, and a later version that wants one starts from D47's confirmation Rule rather than from this task. **Its first claim, however, is not deferred — it is a property v1 must hold**, and it is asserted where the tables are: **no entry in the app's spawn tables is `fugaro cancel`, `fugaro budget kill` or any merge verb**, in V1.1's mutation-proof shape. That assertion is **V1.1's**, and phase V6 does not re-make it.

What follows is D47's recommendation, made concrete as **a refusal — of the app's own path, and of nothing else.** Two claims, and they are not the same claim:

1. **The app spawns none of them.** v1 ships the shape D2 already chose for its deferred writes — the console shows the **exact command**, copyable, the operator runs it, and the CLI's own confirmation and admin check stay in the path **where it has them** — `internal/cli/budget.go`'s `confirmTyped` and `internal/budget/kill.go`'s `AdminDeniedText` for the budget acts, and **nothing for `fugaro cancel`**, which has no typed confirmation at all (design F22), so a voice path to cancel would owe one rather than inherit it. This task's test asserts exactly that: **no entry in the app's spawn tables is `fugaro cancel`, `fugaro budget kill` or any merge verb**, in V1.1's mutation-proof shape.
2. **The agent can still run them, and no part of this plan can say otherwise** (design F38). `claude` has a Bash tool and the operator's own identity; the spec's §8 sends destructive commands "to the agent as normal"; and a misheard turn reaches it as text with the grace period at its default of **0**. The app's read-only guarantee is a statement about the app's argv, and **V6.3 does not, and must not, claim to fence the agent.**

What this task therefore produces, beyond the refusal, is **the honest account**: the document says where a destructive act can originate, that D47's confirmation rule governs only the app's own path, and that D54's permission callbacks plus the operator's deny list are the only thing that limits the other. If the owner later wants a voice command of the app's own, this document is the design it must satisfy, and the rule is stated so it cannot be designed away: **the confirmation must be a different act from the command, and the same misrecognition must not satisfy both** — the read-back names the run id or the argv rather than a paraphrase; the confirming utterance is a fixed short phrase rather than "yes"; it is recognised by a second independent pass with its own confidence floor; and the thing that executes is the CLI's own command, so the CLI's confirmation and admin check stay in the path.
*Why security-sensitive:* every alternative here ends in a merge or in a repository's spend being killed by a misrecognition, and the wording of the guarantee is what a reader will rely on — a claim that reads as covering the agent when it covers only the app is worse than no claim.
*Files:* `console/` (the command display, the tests), `docs/design/voice.md`.
*Verification:* the console's test command (§3's prerequisite), plus the mutation-proof test above **worded as the app's tables and checked against F38's claim**, plus a review criterion: no sentence in `console/README.md` or in this plan states or implies that the agent cannot run a destructive command.
*Independent merge:* yes.
