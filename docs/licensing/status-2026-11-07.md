# Licensing (open core): the census of the implementation plan, 2026-11-07

**Status: a census, not a plan and not an approval.** It reads every task of
[implementation-plan.md](implementation-plan.md) against `origin/main` at `0c705ec8` (2026-11-07) with
`git log` and `git grep` on that commit — never a commit title alone — and records what the tree
holds. It approves no task, reopens no decision, and changes no sentence of the plan: the owner's
0.23.0 scope of 2026-11-07 names this work, and approving a task is a separate act.

**States.** *DONE* — the task's outcome is in the tree, with the commit and the test or file that
proves it. *PARTIAL* — some of its deliverables are in the tree and the rest are named. *NOT
STARTED* — nothing of it is in the tree. *OWNER-RUN* — the task is the owner's own; the cell says
whether its result is recorded in the repository's memory-free check register,
[`docs/gcp-live-checklist.md`](../gcp-live-checklist.md), or only in prose.

**Scope.** The tasks of `docs/licensing/implementation-plan.md` (L0.1–L5.2). The folder's other
documents are not plans: [spec.md](spec.md) is the owner's spec, [reconciliation.md](reconciliation.md)
its reconciliation, [dependency-audit.md](dependency-audit.md) an audit, and
[subscription-terms.md](subscription-terms.md) the agreement text.
`docs/subscriptions/implementation-plan.md` is a plan of its own that took three of these over —
S1.6 is carried there as E3.2, S2.1 supersedes L3.1 — and its README status line is its own, not
this census's.
