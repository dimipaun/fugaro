- An installation that runs the coordinator's job now keeps its licence up to date on its own: `fugaro update` creates the renewal job and a daily schedule for it, so a licence does not lapse between visits.
- The renewal job runs as an account of its own that can read the key, write the key back and read and add the renewal secret, and nothing else of your project.
- The job is created on every installation that deploys the coordinator's job, whether or not a licence was bought: with no key it does nothing and says nothing, and `fugaro licence set` is what gives it one.

### For operators

1. Upgrade the CLI and run `fugaro update` once per installation. It applies the licence job, its schedule, its account and the empty container the renewal secret is written into — with no question on a managed project, and everywhere else inside the confirmation `update` already asks for a plan that creates something.
2. Nothing else. An installation with no licence bought yet needs no step: the job runs daily, finds no key and stops.

### Known limits

- An installation that has not turned the coordinator's job on gets no renewal job — the job runs the coordinator's image, which such an installation does not have. `fugaro licence status` says renewal is by hand there.
- The job renews a licence whose renewal secret the installation holds. A key that arrived without one is renewed by hand, by pasting the next key with `fugaro licence set`.
