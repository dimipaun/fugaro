# Fugaro Console 0.117.0 "Pear"

Console 0.116.0 got the feel of its tabs wrong: the Graph's "no checkout" refusal stayed on screen under every other tab while that tab's own read ran, and Spend, Projects and Coordinator were slow, gave no sign that a click had been taken, and could show a stale view. Pear fixes that, and redesigns the Graph's canvas.

- **Switching tabs clears at once:** a tab no longer keeps the last tab's drawing until its own read answers, so the Graph's "no checkout" refusal never sits under Spend, Project or Coordinator. When there is no checkout, the Graph says so on a calm card instead of a raw CLI error.
- **A skeleton while each tab loads:** every tab shows the shape of its content, section by section, while its data is read, and each section is replaced the moment its own read lands.
- **Spend answers in the same frame:** changing the window or the breakdown shows a busy mark at once over the figures already drawn, keeps your latest choice when clicks overlap, shows a view it has already read straight away with an "as of" time, and draws the caps and the report each as it arrives instead of waiting for the slow report.
- **Each part drawn as its data arrives:** Project, Coordinator, Graph and Live draw their fast part first and fill in the rest when the slower read lands. Project makes one fast read and no longer lists jobs; Coordinator shows its header at once and no longer prints "declared by" followed by a redacted address.
- **A redesigned Graph canvas:** streams are drawn as rows, each task is a card with its full title and a border that shows its state, and the critical edges are marked.
- **The "Projects" tab is "Project":** it is the settings of this one project.
- **Download the build you need:** the `.dmg` (macOS, signed and notarised), or the AppImage or the `.deb` (Linux, **unsigned**: an AppImage is not code-signed, there is no Linux signing key and none is planned; the SHA-256 in `console-sha256sums.txt` is what proves the bytes, and that file is signed with the Sigstore bundle beside it). There is no Windows download.

### For operators

- On macOS, console 0.116.0 offers this update by itself: press **Restart to update** when the notice names 0.117.0. This is the first release the 0.116.0 updater can install. On Linux, download the AppImage or the `.deb` from this release's page. There is nothing else to do.

### Known limits

- The Graph's canvas is redesigned, but the rest of the Graph screen and the Coordinator screen keep their previous look; their redesign follows in a later release.
- On the Project screen Kill, Revive and Add cap are drawn but disabled, and Halt fleet, Halt project and Reap stay disabled: launching is still the console's only write.
- The Caps today panel does not show per-repository compute spend yet.
- The console reads a day of runs and draws the newest rows only; it does not yet ask the CLI for a limited listing.
- Launching needs Fugaro 0.11.2 or later, and the leased split on the cap bar needs Fugaro 0.13.0 or later.
- Windows comes in a later release.
- The Fugaro Subscription Terms that the licence refers to may not be published yet.
