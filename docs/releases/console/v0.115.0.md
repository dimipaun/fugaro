# Fugaro Console 0.115.0 "Papaya"

The Spend screen and the Run detail are redesigned, and the console now shows everything it can read from Fugaro 0.12, 0.13 and every newer release.

- **A redesigned Spend screen:** range and grouping controls, totals that keep billed, list-price and compute money apart, one grouped bar chart and a table, and a Caps today panel that shows the global cap and each repository's cap with every figure under its own label.
- **A redesigned Run detail:** a header and status strip, a ledger that shows what the run was charged and what its plan paid, and the run's pull request.
- **Newer Fugaro releases work in full:** the console accepts any Fugaro release newer than the ones it was built against and shows everything it can read, instead of falling back to reduced mode.
- **The macOS app icon has Apple's rounded shape** in the Dock and in Finder, instead of a full square.
- **A version notice you can dismiss:** when the console runs in reduced mode, a short card says why, with the details one click away, and a Reduced mode chip in the header stays visible and brings the card back.
- **Download the build you need:** the `.dmg` (macOS, signed and notarised), or the AppImage or the `.deb` (Linux, **unsigned**: the SHA-256 in `console-sha256sums.txt` is what proves the bytes, and that file is signed with the Sigstore bundle beside it).

### Known limits

- The Projects, Graph and Coordinator screens keep their previous look; their redesign follows in 0.116.0.
- Automatic updates arrive in 0.116.0, and Windows comes in a later release.
- With Fugaro 0.9 or 0.10, a prerelease or a development build, the console runs in reduced mode: spend, caps and the kill controls are hidden.
- Launching needs Fugaro 0.11.2 or later, and the leased split on the cap bar needs Fugaro 0.13.0 or later.
- The Caps today panel does not show per-repository compute spend yet.
- Halt fleet, Halt project and Reap are drawn but disabled: launching is still the console's only write.
- The Fugaro Subscription Terms that the licence refers to may not be published yet.
