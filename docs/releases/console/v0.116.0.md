# Fugaro Console 0.116.0 "Peach"

The Projects screen is redesigned, and on macOS the console now keeps itself up to date: it downloads a newer console quietly and installs it only when you press Restart to update.

- **A redesigned Projects screen:** one caps table with the global row, the repository defaults and a row per repository, each with its daily cap, its per-run cap and where the cap comes from; a kill switch for the project and for each repository, showing whether it is live or killed and who killed it, when and why; the repositories as cards that say whether each one has a cap row, has run, or is running with no cap row; and the policy and enrollment panels as locked cards that say why they are locked.
- **Automatic updates on macOS:** the signed app checks the console's own stable releases at launch and every six hours and downloads a newer version in the background. Nothing is installed until you press **Restart to update** on the notice that names the version, the update is never installed on quit or while a launch is in progress, and only the version the notice named is installed. Builds you run from source and the Linux builds do not update themselves.
- **An off switch for updates:** **Check for Updates Automatically** in the application menu turns the checks on and off, and **Check for Updates Now** checks at once. The setting is kept in `updates.json` in the console's own data folder, and setting `FUGARO_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1` turns the checks off as well. If the console cannot set the updater up safely, it says so in its log, turns updates off and starts as usual.
- **Where updates are kept:** a download goes to `~/Library/Caches/fugaro-console-updater`, and Squirrel.Mac keeps its own staging folder while it installs; both appear only after a download. Nothing in either place identifies your machine, and no update request carries an identifier.
- **Newer Fugaro releases still work in full:** as in 0.115.0, the console shows everything it can read from Fugaro 0.12 and every newer release, and runs in reduced mode with Fugaro 0.9 or 0.10, a prerelease or a development build.
- **Download the build you need:** the `.dmg` (macOS, signed and notarised), or the AppImage or the `.deb` (Linux, **unsigned**: an AppImage is not code-signed, there is no Linux signing key and none is planned; the SHA-256 in `console-sha256sums.txt` is what proves the bytes, and that file is signed with the Sigstore bundle beside it). There is no Windows download.

### For operators

- Console 0.115.0 and every earlier release has no updater, so install 0.116.0 by hand: download the `.dmg` from this release's page and replace the app. From 0.116.0 on, new console releases arrive in the app. There is no other step.

### Known limits

- The Graph and Coordinator screens keep their previous look; their redesign follows in a later release.
- On the Projects screen Kill, Revive and Add cap are drawn but disabled, and Halt fleet, Halt project and Reap stay disabled: launching is still the console's only write.
- The Caps today panel does not show per-repository compute spend yet.
- Launching needs Fugaro 0.11.2 or later, and the leased split on the cap bar needs Fugaro 0.13.0 or later.
- Windows comes in a later release.
- The Fugaro Subscription Terms that the licence refers to may not be published yet.
